## 1. Top Cultural Highlights: Ancient Empires and Living Heritage (Siem Reap & Phnom Penh)

No first journey to Cambodia is complete without greeting the dawn at **Siem Reap**, home to the UNESCO World Heritage **Angkor Archaeological Park**. Witnessing the sunrise silhouette against the towering stone lotus towers of Angkor Wat is a bucket-list spectacle. Wander deeper into the jungle-embraced roots of Ta Prohm and gaze into the serene, enigmatic smiling faces carved into the stone of the Bayon Temple.

A scenic southward journey brings you to **Phnom Penh**, Cambodia’s vibrant capital city set at the confluence of the mighty Mekong and Tonle Sap rivers. Stroll down the riverside promenade, marvel at the classic Khmer architecture and sacred emerald Buddha at the **Royal Palace & Silver Pagoda**, and savor authentic street delicacies like *Nom Banh Chok* (traditional Khmer noodles) and aromatic *Fish Amok*.

## ២. តំបន់ធម្មជាតិដ៏ស្រស់ត្រកាល (មណ្ឌលគិរី និង កំពត)
## 2. Hidden Nature Gems: Mist-Veiled Highlands and Riverside Serenity (Mondulkiri & Kampot)

Beyond the temples lies an untouched natural sanctuary waiting to be explored. Head east into the pine-covered hills of **Mondulkiri**, where cool highland breezes replace tropical heat. Here, you can walk alongside rescued Asian elephants in ethical, community-run sanctuaries, trek through evergreen canopies, and marvel at the roaring multi-tiered cascades of Bousra Waterfall.

For a slow-paced, tranquil escape, journey south to **Kampot**. Nestled peacefully beside the Praek Tuek Chhu river with Mount Bokor looming on the horizon, Kampot captivates visitors with French colonial shophouses, tranquil sunset river cruises to spot dancing fireflies, and world-renowned organic Kampot pepper plantations. Take a quick day-trip to neighboring Kep to feast on sweet steamed blue crabs paired with fresh green peppercorns right at the water’s edge.

## 3. Tropical Islands: Turquoise Seas and Pristine Sands (Koh Rong)

When you are ready to unwind, Cambodia’s southwestern coastline reveals some of Asia’s most pristine island retreats. **Koh Rong** and its sister island, **Koh Rong Sanloem**, boast miles of blindingly white, powder-soft sands framed by emerald coconut palms and crystalline waters. 

Spend your days diving among vibrant coral reefs, kayaking through mangrove forests, or lounging in secluded luxury overwater bungalows. As twilight falls, witness a magical marine phenomenon: swim amidst glowing bioluminescent plankton that turns the night ocean into an ethereal starry sky.

## Essential Travel Tips for First-Time Visitors

- **Best Season to Visit:** November through March brings pleasant temperatures and minimal rainfall, ideal for temple exploration and beach leisure.
- **Currency & Payments:** US Dollars (USD) and Cambodian Riel (KHR) are universally accepted. Digital payments via **KHQR** or local bank apps (like ABA Mobile) are widespread throughout cafes, hotels, and markets.
- **Cultural Etiquette:** When visiting sacred pagodas and temples, dress respectfully by keeping your shoulders and knees covered. A gentle palm press greeting (*Sampeah*) alongside a warm smile opens every door in Cambodia.
